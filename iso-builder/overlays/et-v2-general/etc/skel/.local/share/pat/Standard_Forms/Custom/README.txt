Drop your OWN Winlink forms here (or use: li-winlink-forms add <files>).

A form = a .txt template (+ its Initial/Viewer .html pages). Pat discovers
every .txt template under Standard_Forms, including this folder. Forms placed
here are NOT overwritten by Pat's "Update Forms" (which only refreshes the
official folders). A master copy is also kept in
~/.config/liaisonos/winlink-forms/ — run 'li-winlink-forms sync' to restore
these if they ever go missing after an update.
