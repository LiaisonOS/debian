PAREDN Communication Frequency Form v1.1

Customizable Title & Subtitle
Click directly on the large title or subtitle text at the top to edit it. Changes are saved to your browser for next time.

Customizable Column Names
Click any yellow header cell and type a new column name. The column name is sent in the message and shown on the viewer form.

Resizable Columns
Hover over the right edge of any yellow column header -- the cursor becomes a resize arrow. Drag left or right to adjust that column’s width. Click Reset Col Widths to restore defaults. Column widths are saved when you use Save Form Data.

Data Rows (20 entries)
Default columns: Description/Channel, Tactical/Net Name, Frequency (MHz), Mode, Offset/Duplex, PL/DCS Tone, Net Control, Remarks/Location.

Save & Load
Use Save Form Data to save a .txt file that can be reloaded, preserving all data including column widths. Use Export to CSV for spreadsheet use.
