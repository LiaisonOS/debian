Winlink PH SMS Message
This form generates a short text message that can be sent to an email and/or SMS address.
The PAREDN club (DX1PRD) runs SMS gateways for PH, US, and AU/VK numbers - accessible via Winlink only.
No delivery guarantee is made or implied.
Send to address(s):
Separate multiple email addresses and SMS numbers with a comma ( , )
Local PH SMS number(s):  11 digits starting with 0 each, comma-separated, max 15 numbers (e.g. 09171234567, 09181234567)
US & Puerto Rico SMS number(s):  Include country code without + sign, comma-separated, max 15 numbers (e.g. 12125551234 for US, 17872345678 for Puerto Rico)
Australian SMS number(s):  Australian mobile numbers, comma-separated, max 15 (e.g. 0412345678 or 61412345678)
 ⚠ AU recipients must opt-in at aprs.wiki before first message. Delivered via APRS-IS / NA7Q SMS Gateway.
